Doorstep

Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Doorstep helps small landlords track rent, bills and repairs. This page explains what we store, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Your property data: the properties, units, tenants, bills, payments, repairs, documents and photos you or your co-owners add.
  • From tenants: repair requests, photos and “I’ve paid” notes they send through their private link.
  • If you turn them on: push-notification addresses for your devices, and the bill and Zelle emails your own Gmail script forwards.

Who can see it

  • Only the people who are members of a property can see that property’s data.
  • A tenant’s private link shows only that tenant’s rent, pay instructions and their own requests.
  • We don’t sell your data or show ads.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the data and handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Groq runs the AI helper when you use it. Questions and the relevant numbers are sent; account numbers, phone numbers and emails are not.
  • National Weather Service and Open-Meteo provide weather for the address you enter.

The demo

The demo account is shared by everyone who tries it and is erased and rebuilt every night. Don’t put real information in it.

Your choices

  • Download: Settings › Your data › Download my data gives you a copy of a property’s data.
  • Delete: Settings › Your data › Delete my account removes your login and any property no one else shares with you, including its photos and files.
  • Tenant links can be turned off by the landlord at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above.
